Core Technology

Five engineering pillars

🌍

Passive Geothermal Core

At 5m underground, soil temperature stays stable at ~18°C year-round. Hot 44°C summer air passes through and exits at 22–26°C — no compressor needed for the primary cooling load.

Zero energy cooling
🔬

Medical-Grade HEPA Filtration

Multi-stage filtration eliminates PM2.5 particles, crop-burning smoke, fine pollen, and airborne pathogens. The air entering your home is cleaner than most hospitals.

Air purification
☀️

Solar-Battery Integration

A 5 kW solar array with 10–15 kWh lithium battery fully powers the entire system. The ultra-low 1–1.5 kW load means near-zero electricity cost year-round.

Energy independence
🏠

Smart Floor-Wise Zoning

Motorized dampers direct conditioned air only to occupied zones — living rooms by day, bedrooms at night. Maximum comfort from minimum energy.

Intelligent zoning
💧

Gravity Condensate Management

1–2% incline slope drains condensation automatically into a slump chamber with a submersible auto-pump. Zero water stagnation, zero mold, zero odor.

Self-maintaining
🔄

Continuous Air Replacement

A central vertical riser continuously exhausts stale hot air from every floor. No recirculation, no CO2 buildup, no stuffiness — ever. Your home stays oxygen-rich 24/7.

100% fresh air
